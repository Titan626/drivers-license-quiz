## Duplicate review

Compared each new question with all 100 existing questions and their explanations, including related concepts:

- Right-hand bend recognition is distinct from the compulsory right turn in question 4 and the prohibited right turn in question 84.
- General carriageway narrowing is distinct from question 44's specific narrow-bridge warning.
- A T-junction has three approaches, distinct from question 45's four-way crossroads and the interchange designs in questions 73–74.
- The blue compulsory U-turn is distinct from question 9's red-bordered U-turn prohibition.
- Rest-area information is new; existing information-sign questions identify a hospital or airport.
- Red-and-amber together is distinct from question 16's amber-only signal.
- Turning across pedestrians at a green signal is distinct from question 19's zebra crossing and question 87's blocked junction exit.
- Obscured overtaking at a hill crest, making room for an ambulance and recovering from a missed expressway exit are new decisions. They do not repeat overtaking side, being overtaken, expressway access restrictions or reversing from a side road.
- Clutch operation, hydraulic brake-fluid purpose, securing a parked vehicle and failed wiper-blade clearing add specific controls/maintenance facts beyond the existing pre-trip checklist questions.
- Aquaplaning tests immediate recovery from loss of tyre grip; question 28 tests precautions before or during heavy rain.
- Drowsiness tests acting on current impairment; questions 22 and 77 test scheduled driving/rest limits.
- Medicine warnings, crash-helmet use, covering loose cargo and safe responses to animals are new Highway Code objectives.

Automated checks verify that the 20 new stems and correct-answer strings do not duplicate existing entries or one another. Shared broad subject areas are intentional; each addition tests a different sign or decision.

## Scope of source verification

FRSC establishes Nigerian sign/signal meanings, daily vehicle checks, helmet use and covering unstable materials. UK Department for Transport and NHTSA references support general safe-driving techniques, and Toyota supports hydraulic brake-system operation. International law, lane-side rules, penalties and numeric limits are not imported into the Nigerian questions. Vehicle-specific parking and maintenance procedures remain governed by the vehicle handbook. Worn-wiper replacement is the practical corrective action inferred from FRSC's requirement for effective blades.

This batch verifies the 20 new answers; it does not revalidate every older question.
