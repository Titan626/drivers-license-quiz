## Duplicate review

Reviewed every existing question and explanation before drafting. Compared learning objectives as well as wording:

- Give Way tests yielding without an automatic full stop, distinct from question 1’s STOP instruction.
- No Stopping tests the two-diagonal sign; question 6 tests the one-diagonal No Parking sign.
- Minimum speed tests a blue mandatory number, distinct from question 7’s red-bordered maximum.
- No Right Turn tests prohibition; question 4 tests a compulsory right turn and question 9 prohibits a U-turn.
- Airport direction adds a new information pictogram rather than repeating the hospital sign.
- Chevron exclusion, blocked green junctions, filter arrows, blind spots and officer control are new traffic-rule learning objectives.
- Cold pressure measurement, worn-to-indicator tread, functioning ABS, interior demisting and the oil dipstick are new vehicle-maintenance/control objectives; these are not rewordings of tyre blowout, rain driving, pre-trip check lists or oil-pressure emergencies.
- Defensive driving, the emergency telephone number, training-car L signs, reversing into a major road and night glare are new Highway Code learning objectives.

Shared subject areas are intentional; no new question repeats an existing tested fact. Automated checks verify unique new stems and correct-answer combinations. Original generic road-sign stems can repeat because their pictures and answers differ.

## Scope of answer verification

FRSC sources establish Nigerian sign, signal, road-marking, training-car and emergency-number facts. NHTSA supports general tyre and functioning-ABS guidance; Ontario’s transport ministry supports the general shoulder-check technique. Those international safety sources are not presented as Nigerian licensing laws. Manufacturer-specific maintenance procedures remain governed by each vehicle handbook. This expansion verifies the 20 new answers; it is not a full revalidation of older questions.
